⚠ Google Ads Diagnosis

8 Reasons Your Google Ads
Stopped Generating Leads

You've been spending budget for 8–10 years with near-zero conversions in recent years. Here's the exact root cause analysis — and the fix for each.

01
Unstructured Historical Account Data
Years of messy campaigns, paused ad groups, duplicate keywords, and mixed match types are actively poisoning Google's Smart Bidding algorithm. The AI is learning from bad data — so it keeps making bad decisions and targeting the wrong people.
Full account audit, clean restructure, archive legacy campaigns. Start fresh within the same account so new clean data teaches Google's AI correctly.
02
Zero Conversion Tracking Setup
Without conversion tracking, Google has no signal of what a "good" outcome looks like. So it optimises for cheap clicks — from students, researchers, competitors — not actual B2B manufacturers looking for certification services.
Set up Google Tag Manager + conversion events for: form submissions, WhatsApp clicks, phone calls, email clicks. Enable value-based bidding once data accumulates.
03
Broad Match Keywords Burning Budget
Broad match keywords like "certification" or "testing services" pull in millions of irrelevant searches. B2C consumers, job seekers, and students all click — costing you money with zero chance of converting into a B2B client.
Switch to Phrase Match + Exact Match for all core B2B intent keywords. Build a negative keyword list blocking B2C, informational, and irrelevant queries immediately.
04
No Remarketing — Warm Leads Lost Forever
B2B buyers research for weeks before contacting. Every qualified manufacturer who visited your website and left without converting is gone forever — because you have no remarketing to bring them back when they're ready to buy.
Install Google Ads Remarketing tag. Build 3 audiences: All visitors (90 days), High-dwell visitors (3+ min), Form abandoners (14 days). Show tailored ads to each segment.
05
No B2B Audience Segmentation
Showing the same ad to everyone wastes budget. A student searching "what is EAC certification" and an Export Manager searching "EAC certification service for electrical goods India" should never see the same ad — but they currently do.
Create separate campaigns per audience segment, product type, and geography. Use In-Market B2B audiences + Custom Intent audiences built from your best-converting keyword themes.
06
Landing Pages Misaligned with Ad Copy
When ad copy promises "EAC Certification for Electrical Goods" but the landing page is a generic homepage, Google drops your Quality Score. Higher CPC, lower ad position, fewer impressions — a triple punishment that kills campaign efficiency.
Build dedicated landing pages per campaign: one per certification type (EAC, TR CU, GOST), one per target country. Match headline, copy, and CTA exactly to the ad that sent the visitor.
07
Missing B2B Ad Extensions
You're likely running bare-bones ads without Sitelinks, Callouts, Call extensions, or Lead Form extensions. This shrinks your ad's visual footprint, lowers CTR, and misses easy ways to pre-qualify leads before they even click.
Add all relevant extensions: Sitelinks (services, industries, contact), Callout extensions (certifications offered), Call extension (direct phone), Lead Form extension for instant enquiries from the SERP itself.
08
No Demand Gen — Missed Google AI Advantage
After fixing Search campaigns, the next growth lever is Demand Gen — Google's AI-powered campaign type that surfaces your ads to in-market B2B audiences across YouTube, Discover, Gmail, and Display. You're currently missing this entirely.
After 60 days of clean conversion data, activate Demand Gen campaigns. Google's AI will find new EAEU-focused B2B buyers you'd never reach via Search alone — at a significantly lower CPL.

🔍 SEO Audit Findings

9 Critical SEO Issues Found
on Your Website

During our initial technical and on-page review of your website, these issues were identified. Each one is actively preventing you from ranking on Google globally — or locally in any specific country.

🇰🇷
CRITICAL
Default Language Set to Korean
Your website's default language is Korean (set in HTML lang tag, meta data, and likely server config). Google reads this and primarily shows your site to Korean-language users — severely limiting your global reach. Any English content you have is being undervalued by search engines.
Fix Required: Set lang="en" as default. If multilingual is needed, implement proper hreflang tags: hreflang="en" for global English, hreflang="ko" for Korean, hreflang="ru" for Russia. This signals to Google which version to show in which country — critical for EAEU market ranking.
🔗
CRITICAL
URL Structure Not Optimised
URLs appear to be unstructured — likely dynamic URLs with parameters (e.g. ?page=23&id=45) or Korean-language slugs. Google cannot read or rank pages when the URL doesn't describe the content. Poor URL structure = poor rankings, poor crawlability, and lower user trust.
Fix Required: Implement clean, descriptive English URLs: /eac-certification-services/, /tr-cu-certification-russia/, /eaeu-market-entry/. Use hyphens, keep them short, include primary keyword. Redirect old URLs with 301 to new ones.
📝
CRITICAL
Very Thin Content on All Pages
Each service page has very little content — likely under 200–400 words. Google's algorithm requires depth and topical authority to rank competitive B2B keywords. Thin pages signal low-quality content and are actively penalised in rankings. Your competitors with 1,000–2,000 word pages will always outrank you.
Fix Required: Each service page needs minimum 800–1,200 words of rich, structured, keyword-relevant content: what the certification is, why exporters need it, your process, timelines, costs, FAQ. This alone can move rankings significantly within 60–90 days.
🚫
CRITICAL
Content Copying Blocked (Right-Click Disabled)
Your website uses JavaScript to prevent users from copying text. While intended to protect content, this is a well-known SEO anti-pattern. Search engine crawlers sometimes struggle with JavaScript-heavy copy-blocking. More critically, it prevents journalists, partners, and directory sites from easily quoting or referencing your content — killing natural backlink opportunities.
Fix Required: Remove the copy-block JavaScript entirely. Instead, add proper metadata, copyright notice in footer, and if concerned about plagiarism, use Google Search Console's "Request Removal" for scrapers. Allowing copying = more citations = more backlinks = higher rankings.
⚡
HIGH PRIORITY
Technical SEO Issues & Core Web Vitals
Without a full crawl we can confirm there will be common technical issues: slow page load speed, unoptimised images, missing or duplicate meta titles/descriptions, improper heading hierarchy (H1/H2/H3), missing schema markup, and broken internal links. These are direct ranking signals Google measures.
Fix Required: Full technical SEO audit via Screaming Frog + Google Search Console. Fix: image compression, lazy loading, browser caching, CDN setup, meta tag optimisation on every page, proper H1 structure (one unique H1 per page), schema markup for B2B service pages.
☣️
HIGH PRIORITY
Rising Website Spam Score
Your domain has a rising spam score — likely caused by low-quality inbound links, spammy directory submissions, or link schemes from the past. If the spam score continues to rise, Google will devalue your entire domain. If it crosses a critical threshold, your rankings across all pages will drop sharply or disappear entirely.
Fix Required: Run full backlink audit via Ahrefs / SEMrush. Identify toxic/spammy backlinks. Submit Google Disavow file for confirmed spam links. Begin replacing with quality editorial backlinks from relevant B2B and trade publications. Act now — the score is still in a manageable range.
🌍
ON-PAGE FIX
No Geo-Targeted Content for EAEU Countries
You want to rank for searches from India, UAE, Europe for EAEU-related queries. But currently there are no country-specific landing pages or localised content. A company in India searching "EAC certification India" vs one in Germany searching the same should land on content that speaks to their specific context.
Fix Required: Create geo-targeted landing pages: /eac-certification-india/, /eaeu-certification-uae/. Use Local SEO principles per target country. Include country-specific trust signals, local currency pricing where relevant, and local contact details.
📑
ON-PAGE FIX
Missing On-Page SEO Fundamentals
Across all pages: meta titles are likely not keyword-optimised or are identical across pages, meta descriptions are missing or auto-generated, H1 tags are missing or contain generic text, image alt tags are empty or in Korean, and internal linking structure is weak with pages not pointing to each other strategically.
Fix Required: Manually optimise every key page: unique keyword-rich title tag (50–60 chars), compelling meta description (140–160 chars), one focused H1, keyword in first 100 words of content, 2–3 internal links per page, all image alt tags in English describing the image with relevant keywords.
🔄
OFF-PAGE FIX
Weak Backlink Profile
For competitive B2B keywords in the TIC and EAEU certification space, domain authority built through quality backlinks is essential. Your current backlink profile (combined with the rising spam score) is not strong enough to compete with established TIC companies and international certification bodies that dominate page 1.
Fix Required: Build 10–15 quality backlinks per month from: B2B trade directories (Export Promotion Councils, Chamber of Commerce listings), industry publications, EAEU/Russia trade news sites, guest posts on export/manufacturing blogs, PR mentions on business news portals. Quality over quantity — 5 DA60+ links beat 50 spam links.
