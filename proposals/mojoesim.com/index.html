🇺🇸 USA Strategy — Special Note

Why We're Not Running
Standard Google Ads in USA Yet

The USA is the world's largest eSIM market — but it requires a completely different approach. Here's the data-driven rationale.

🇺🇸

United States — Deferred to Month 3+ (P-Max Only)

Economics do not support direct Search bidding with current account data and eSIM price points

⏳ Month 3+
01
CPC vs Product Price Mismatch
Top-of-page CPCs in the USA for eSIM keywords typically range from $8–$25+ per click. With eSIM plans often priced at $8–$25 for a data bundle, a single ad click could cost as much as — or more than — the product itself. The unit economics simply don't work for direct Search campaigns without a proven conversion rate above 15–20%, which takes months to achieve.
02
No Account Conversion History
Google's Smart Bidding (Target CPA, Max Conversions) requires a minimum of 30–50 conversions per month to learn effectively. Without this baseline, Google wastes budget on high-CPC clicks from users with no real intent. Running USA campaigns on a fresh account with no conversion data would result in CPAs of ₹3,000–8,000 per sale — unviable.
03
Extremely High Competition
The US eSIM space is dominated by well-funded players with enormous brand recognition. eSIM companies and major telcos have been running high-budget Google Ads in the US for years, pushing CPCs to levels that make entry without a proven ROAS model financially risky for a brand building its data profile.
04
P-Max Changes the Math
Performance Max campaigns use Google's AI to find buyers across every surface — including YouTube, Gmail, Discover, and Display — at a significantly lower CPL than Search alone. Once we have 60–90 days of clean conversion data from European campaigns, Google's AI will know exactly what a MojoeSIM buyer looks like and can find them in the USA at a fraction of the Search CPC. This is the right entry path for USA.
🤖

USA Entry Plan — Month 3: Performance Max Campaign

At Month 3, we launch a P-Max campaign for USA with asset groups built around your best-converting European creatives and buyer data. Google's AI will find US-based travellers who look like your European buyers — at a far better CPC than manual Search bidding. We start with a small test budget of ₹5,000–8,000/month, measure CPL vs. product price, and scale only when ROAS is proven positive. No budget wasted, no guesswork — just AI doing what it does best when given real data to work with.

🎯 Match Type Strategy

Broad + Phrase + Exact —
How Each Match Type Earns Its Place

All three match types run simultaneously but with very different roles, budgets, and purposes. This is not a set-it-and-forget-it setup — match types are reviewed weekly and adjusted based on search term reports.

Broad Match
Data Discovery Layer
Purpose: Find keyword gold mines we haven't thought of
  • Small budget (15–20% of total per campaign) with very aggressive negatives
  • Uncovers unexpected high-converting search terms from real users
  • Every winner from broad goes into Phrase or Exact immediately
  • Reviewed every 3–4 days — any irrelevant match gets a negative added
  • Budget capped per day to prevent runaway spend on irrelevant terms
Phrase Match
Core Workhorse
Purpose: Capture intent variations of your key themes
  • 50–60% of each campaign's budget runs here
  • Catches all variations: "best esim for italy", "cheap italy esim", "italy travel esim"
  • Allows for creative flexibility in ad copy matching query intent
  • Weekly search term report review — winners go exact, losers get negatives
  • Builds conversion volume needed to enable Smart Bidding faster
Exact Match
Conversion Precision
Purpose: Lock in proven keywords at lower CPC
  • 20–30% of budget — extremely tight, only proven converters go here
  • Highest Quality Score = lowest CPC + top ad position
  • Ad copy written specifically for that one exact query
  • Enables Target CPA bidding per keyword as data accumulates
  • Grows over time as winning terms migrate from Phrase match

🖥 Landing Page Optimisation

What MojoeSIM's Landing Pages
Need to Convert Paid Traffic

Paid traffic is wasted if the landing page doesn't match the ad's promise and guide the visitor straight to purchase. These are the highest-impact improvements — most can be implemented quickly without a full redesign.

🔗
MUST HAVE
Dedicated Country Landing Pages
Every country campaign (UK, DE, FR, NL, IT) must link to a dedicated landing page — not the homepage. The page headline, subheading, and intro paragraph must match the ad's promise. "UK eSIM from $X — Instant Activation" in the ad = exact same message on the page.
Action: Create or configure: /esim-uk, /esim-germany, /esim-france, /esim-netherlands, /esim-italy. Each pulls the relevant plan cards. H1 matches ad keyword. This alone improves Quality Score by 2–4 points, dropping CPC by 25–40%.
⚡
MUST HAVE
Page Load Speed Under 2.5s
Google's Quality Score directly penalises slow landing pages. A 1-second delay in load time reduces conversions by ~7%. Mobile travellers searching on-the-go abandon pages that don't load instantly. mojoesim.com should hit Core Web Vitals green on mobile.
Action: Compress all images to WebP format. Lazy-load non-critical assets. Enable browser caching. Consider a CDN (Cloudflare free tier). Test via PageSpeed Insights — target 80+ mobile score.
📱
MUST HAVE
Mobile-First Purchase Flow
70–80% of paid eSIM traffic will come from mobile devices — people searching for data plans are often about to travel or are already at the airport. The entire purchase flow — plan selection, payment, eSIM delivery — must be frictionless on mobile.
Action: Test the full purchase flow on iPhone and Android monthly. Ensure Razorpay checkout renders correctly on mobile. CTA buttons must be above the fold and fingertip-sized (min 48px height). Eliminate any horizontal scrolling.
🏆
IMPORTANT
Trust Signals Above the Fold
First-time visitors from paid ads need trust cues within 3 seconds. "190+ countries", "Instant Activation", and "Secure Checkout" are already on the site — but they need to be more visible and prominent on landing pages for ad traffic who may not know the brand.
Action: Add a trust bar below the hero: ✓ 190+ Countries · ✓ Instant QR Activation · ✓ Secure Checkout · ✓ 24/7 Support. Add star rating / review count if available. Show a "How it works in 3 steps" section early on the page.
🔔
IMPORTANT
Urgency & Scarcity Cues
Travellers make impulse purchases. Adding gentle urgency elements — without fake countdowns — helps move browsers to buyers. "Activate before you land", "Used by 10,000+ travellers" type social proof + urgency combinations increase conversion rates measurably.
Action: Add "Activate in under 5 minutes" messaging near CTAs. Show number of plans sold for popular destinations. Use a sticky "Get Your UK eSIM Now →" floating CTA on scroll. These elements typically add 10–20% uplift to conversion rate.
🌐
RECOMMENDED
Localised Content for DE/FR/IT/NL
For German, French, Italian, and Dutch campaigns, landing page copy in the local language significantly improves Quality Score and conversion rate. Users in these markets, particularly Germany and France, respond much better to ads and pages in their native language.
Action: Start with the H1, hero subheading, and CTA button in local language. Run EN vs local-language landing page A/B test for 30 days. If local language converts better (very likely), invest in full page translation for DE and FR first.
